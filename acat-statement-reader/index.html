ACAT Statement Reader

Upload a client's statement from the delivering firm. Claude lists every account with its number, registration, type and positions, and the page checks each value against the statement itself. Check each account, then copy the details into ClientWorks.

You have view access. You can read statements with Claude, browse the team log and clearing firms, copy and export. Saving records and changing the firm table need Contributor access (marked below); ask the page owner to change your access in the Share menu.
