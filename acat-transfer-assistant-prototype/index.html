ACAT Transfer Assistant

ACAT Statement → Transfer Form Assistant (Prototype)

Reads a client's custodial statement and helps you assemble the fields LPL's Account Transfer Form (F2) needs — delivering firm, clearing firm, account number, registration, account type, and assets.

How this works & its limits Statement PDFs with a text layer are read entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded or saved. For image-based (scanned) statements, "Read with Claude" sends images of the pages to Claude on your own Claude account to transcribe the text; you'll be asked to allow it the first time. Records on this page are not saved: export or copy them before you close it. Populated fields are best-effort suggestions only — always verify every field against the source statement before using it on a real transfer form. This tool does not submit anything to LPL, Schwab, or anyone else.
